OGS8 Composite Building Layout

Full composite CAD layout designed from scratch for OGS8, a one-story distribution center. Starting from Amazon capacity targets, every process area was sized and placed: an inbound receiving dock with floor-level staging lanes, a ship dock and outbound sortation staging, standard bulk racking (96 x 31 uprights, 5,093 bays), two VNA narrow-aisle rack configurations at 30 and 66 inch aisle widths (561 combined bays), 282 medium-load storage units, associate pick paths routed through every aisle, conveyor routing connecting pick to sortation, and all supporting functional areas. Fulfillment Center Layout · SSD

SIL8 Sub Same-Day FC Layout

Full composite layout for SIL8, a sub same-day delivery fulfillment center. The building is built around an Auto Store robotic grid occupying the central floor area. Robotic Drive Units travel across the top of the grid to retrieve inventory totes and deliver them to stationary pick stations positioned along the grid perimeter. The layout also defines an inbound receiving dock with staging, an outbound ship dock, sortation staging lanes, pallet staging, associate work areas, and all supporting spaces. Physical dimensions, throughput routing, and Amazon Robotics system integration were coordinated from initial capacity inputs through the final Rev 0.1 drawing. Name and date appear in the title block as engineer of record.

Optimization of an Angle of Attack Sensor for Fighter Aircraft

Fluid Mechanics final project at the University of Akron. Redesigned an existing angle of attack sensor to reduce aerodynamic drag while maintaining measurement accuracy. Applied CFD analysis in Autodesk CFD across incompressible (100 m/s) and compressible (400 m/s) flow conditions at 0, 5, and 10 degree angles of attack, comparing drag force and wake characteristics against the baseline design.
